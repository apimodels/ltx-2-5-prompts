## Prompt

````text
A classic ink and grey watercolour newspaper gag cartoon comes to life with small, simple cartoon motion. The camera is fixed on a tripod and does not move, drift, zoom or push in at any point, so the framing at the end of the clip is exactly the framing at the start. The baffled office worker with the big nose and round glasses stays standing in the same spot holding his framed certificate against his chest the whole time: his eyes dart nervously back and forth between the up button and the down button on the wall panel, he leans a little toward the buttons and back again, his raised index finger hovers toward the panel and then pulls back without pressing anything, sweat drops fly off his forehead, and the question mark above his head bobs and wobbles. The elevator doors stay closed. The certificate text, the caption text at the bottom and every letter stay exactly as drawn and never change. Ink linework, crosshatching and flat grey wash are preserved exactly as drawn for the entire clip, with no photorealistic texture, no style drift and no added people, and he never walks or leaves the frame.
````
