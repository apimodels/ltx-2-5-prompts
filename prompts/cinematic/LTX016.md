## Prompt

````text
Use the provided photo reference ONLY for the woman’s identity, face, hair, body appearance, and exact clothing. Do not copy the pose, framing, or background from the reference.
Create a realistic 10-second casual kitchen video.
IMPORTANT CAST RULE:
Only ONE visible person: the woman from the reference.
The man/operator must NEVER appear in frame. He is only heard as an off-screen male voice. At most, ONE of his hands may briefly enter frame to hand over the pumpkin. Never show his face, body, torso, or second hand.
FRAMING:
The camera faces the woman across a kitchen table.
Depth order:
1. closest to camera: the operator’s pumpkin on the near side of the table;
2. behind it: the woman’s pumpkin on the far side of the table;
3. behind both pumpkins: the woman sitting at the table.
The operator’s pumpkin must be clearly visible in the foreground before he gives it to her.
PUMPKIN RULES:
The operator’s pumpkin is hollow and large enough to fit completely over a human head.
Its shape is slightly elongated and oval, a bit watermelon-like, but still clearly a real orange pumpkin.
This pumpkin must have EXACTLY ONE opening only.
That one opening is a simple hole placed low on the front side of the pumpkin.
It is the only cutout in the entire pumpkin.
ABSOLUTE RESTRICTIONS:
- NO eye holes
- NO nose hole
- NO extra holes
- NO triangle cutouts
- NO jack-o-lantern face
- NO carved facial expression
- NO second opening of any kind
- The pumpkin must not look like a face except for that one single low opening
TIMELINE + DIALOGUE:
0.0–2.0s:
The woman sits alone at the kitchen table working on her own pumpkin.
The operator’s pumpkin with exactly one low front opening is visible in the foreground.
The off-screen man says:
“I’m ready.”
She looks toward the camera and replies:
“That fast?”
2.0–3.2s:
The off-screen man says:
“Yeah, try it on.”
Only one male hand enters frame, picks up the foreground pumpkin, and extends it toward her across the table.
3.2–5.3s:
She takes the pumpkin with both hands.
She puts the pumpkin completely over her entire head like a hollow shell. Her whole head goes inside it.
Do NOT place it on top like a hat.
Do NOT leave her face visible.
The pumpkin must cover the top, sides, and back of her head.
5.3–7.5s:
With the pumpkin fully covering her head, she cannot see because there are no eye holes.
She raises both hands and feels the upper front area where eye holes should normally be.
She says:
“But where the eyes?”
7.5–10.0s:
Still wearing the pumpkin over her whole head, she lowers one hand and finds the single low opening.
She touches / points to that opening and says:
“There’s only a mouth here, look.”
The one low opening should be clearly visible when she indicates it.
STYLE:
Natural home video, cozy kitchen lighting, realistic kitchen table, playful casual interaction, believable timing.
CRITICAL:
- Only the woman is visible.
- The man is fully off-screen except possibly one hand.
- Never show a second person sitting at the table.
- The foreground pumpkin has EXACTLY ONE opening.
- That opening is low on the front side.
- Absolutely no eye holes, no nose hole, no other cuts.
- No jack-o-lantern look.
- Pumpkin goes over her entire head, never like a hat.
- Keep dialogue exactly in English.
- One continuous shot, no cuts.
````
