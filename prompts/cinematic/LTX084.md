## Prompt

````text
A stream of amber spirit pours into the rocks glass from off frame right and rises to two thirds, the surface settling as it fills and the caustics on the walnut shifting with it. A single large clear ice cube is lowered in and cracks once, sending a fine line of bubbles up the inside of the glass. Hold the decanter, the bar top, the backlight and the framing of the still exactly as they are, and keep the camera locked off for the entire shot. Sound: the pour, one sharp crack of ice, a bar several metres away with low conversation and no music.
````
