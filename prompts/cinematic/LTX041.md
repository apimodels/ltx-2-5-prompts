## Prompt

````text
Epic photorealistic dark fantasy cinema, 20-second continuous dramatic sequence, inspired by the legendary Bridge of Khazad-dûm confrontation in The Lord of the Rings.

An immense underground dwarven kingdom stretches into absolute darkness. The narrow ancient stone bridge hangs above a terrifying bottomless abyss. Colossal stone pillars disappear into the shadows, enormous rocky vaults rise overhead, and distant orange firelight flickers through drifting smoke and dust.

A realistic anthropomorphic grey cat wizard stands alone on the narrow bridge. His unmistakably feline face has piercing amber eyes, pointed ears, a natural cat muzzle and long white whiskers. He wears weathered grey wizard robes, a pointed grey hat and a flowing cloak. His long white beard moves in the turbulent air.

**0–4 SECONDS — THE CONFRONTATION**

A deep, terrifying rumble echoes through the immense cavern. The feline wizard stares intensely toward an enormous unseen presence beyond the camera.

His cloak ripples in the hot wind. Dust falls from the ancient ceiling. The distant orange glow grows brighter.

The camera slowly pushes forward toward the wizard, emphasizing his determination against the immense darkness.

**4–8 SECONDS — THE WARNING**

The feline wizard straightens his body, tightening his grip on his ancient wooden staff.

His ears flatten, his whiskers tremble, and his amber eyes narrow with fury.

He raises his staff and shouts in a deep, powerful French voice:

"VOUS NE PASSEREZ PAS !!!"

His feline mouth moves naturally in perfect synchronization with the thunderous words. His voice reverberates through the cavern.

The camera accelerates forward into a dramatic low-angle close-up.

**8–12 SECONDS — THE STAFF STRIKE**

With a sudden, tremendous movement, the feline wizard drives his wooden staff violently into the stone bridge.

A brilliant flash of pure white magical light erupts from the point of impact.

A deafening thunderclap shakes the entire cavern.

Deep glowing fractures spread rapidly through the ancient stone bridge, racing forward away from the wizard.

The camera jolts violently from the impact.

**12–16 SECONDS — THE BRIDGE COLLAPSES**

The section of the bridge extending toward the unseen enemy breaks apart.

Gigantic stone blocks split and tumble into the bottomless abyss. The bridge collapses progressively, massive slabs falling into darkness as clouds of dust and debris erupt upward.

The feline wizard remains standing on the surviving section of the bridge, his staff planted firmly in the stone.

The camera rapidly retreats backward, revealing the immense scale of the collapsing bridge and the colossal underground cavern.

A tremendous roar echoes through the darkness, followed by the thunderous sound of falling stone.

**16–20 SECONDS — THE FINAL SHOCKWAVE**

As the bridge collapses, a colossal surge of brilliant white magical energy bursts outward from the fractured stone.

The shockwave races through the cavern toward the retreating camera, carrying dust, glowing sparks and fragments of shattered rock.

The camera is violently propelled farther backward through the enormous underground space, shaking intensely as the destruction spreads.

The expanding magical shockwave overtakes the camera, engulfing the entire lens in a blinding white flash.

The white light rapidly fades into complete blackness.

A final deep rumble echoes through the darkness.

Photorealistic live-action fantasy blockbuster, realistic feline anatomy, natural fur movement, powerful dramatic performance, spectacular environmental destruction, immense architectural scale, realistic stone fracture physics, dramatic volumetric lighting, dynamic cinematic camera choreography, thunderous sound design, epic fantasy atmosphere.
````
