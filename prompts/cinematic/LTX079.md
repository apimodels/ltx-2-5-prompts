## Prompt

````text
Ten seconds, ordinary speed, one continuous shot. 0 to 2 seconds: she holds the set position. The crowd noise drops away. Nothing moves except her breathing. 2 to 5 seconds: the gun. She drives out of the blocks, hands leaving the line, first foot striking behind her, the blocks kicking back slightly. Three long strides with the body still low and the shins driving forward. 5 to 8 seconds: she comes up out of the drive phase into full stride. The camera tracks alongside and slightly ahead, rising to hip height, the track and the lit stadium sliding past behind her. 8 to 10 seconds: the camera settles and holds her at full speed with the stadium lights flaring past. Hold across the take: the same athlete, the kit and its colors, the lane markings and the stadium lighting. Sound: the crowd going quiet, the starter's gun with a stadium echo behind it, spikes striking the track, breathing, the crowd coming back up underneath. Nothing musical. Do not: ramp the speed, add other runners, put numbers or lettering on the kit, or start her before the gun.
````
