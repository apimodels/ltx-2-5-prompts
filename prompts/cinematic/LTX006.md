## Prompt

````text
一.
10-second cinematic animation, 16:9, smooth continuous visual storytelling, elegant camera movement, seamless transition, no hard cuts.**

In a warm sunlit magical forest, a cute round white blob creature receives **one cream-colored letter with a small red heart seal** from a moss-covered mailbox beside its cozy tree-house cottage.

The creature briefly holds the letter, then calmly turns and walks back home along the stone path.

The camera performs a **slow cinematic side-rear tracking shot**, smoothly following the creature at a constant height and direction. Soft foreground flowers and leaves pass close to the lens, creating natural layered parallax. Warm golden sunlight filters through the trees.

As the creature enters the cottage, the **round wooden door frame and hanging leaves smoothly pass across the lens and fully occlude the frame**, creating a natural invisible transition from exterior to interior. Maintain the same camera direction, height, speed, and momentum.

Inside the cozy wooden cottage, the same white creature places the letter safely beside it and begins preparing for a journey.

Keep the packing action **simple, calm, and clearly readable**: the creature puts the final small travel item into a vintage green suitcase, gently closes the suitcase, and secures it. A beige travel hat, small backpack, and camera are ready nearby.

After finishing the packing, the creature becomes still.

It gently picks up **the same letter**, holds it with both hands, and quietly looks at it for a moment.

The camera slowly performs a **subtle cinematic arc and gentle push-in**, moving from the packed suitcase toward the creature and the letter.

The creature remains calm and thoughtful, silently contemplating the message.

End on a warm cinematic medium shot: **the packed suitcase beside the white creature, the letter held carefully in its hands, soft golden light entering through the doorway, everything ready for the coming journey.**

**High-end hand-painted animated feature film, whimsical storybook forest, cozy handcrafted cottage, warm golden-green color palette, soft volumetric sunlight, gentle moving shadows, cinematic depth of field, natural foreground parallax, subtle motion blur, smooth camera inertia, stable character identity, stable letter and suitcase, strong temporal consistency, quiet emotional storytelling, premium cinematic animation quality.**

二.
5-second cinematic video, 16:9, single continuous shot, no cuts.

A vintage moss-covered green steam train moves steadily through a lush sunlit forest. The forest is dense with tall trees, ferns, wildflowers, and soft drifting leaves. Warm golden sunlight shines through the canopy, creating volumetric light rays, moving highlights, and a peaceful magical atmosphere. Thin white steam rises gently from the locomotive.

The camera begins outside the train, smoothly tracking alongside the moving train with stable cinematic motion. The train remains visually consistent and solid. Foreground leaves pass softly near the lens, creating natural parallax and depth.

As the train continues forward, the camera slowly glides closer to a passenger window while maintaining the same movement direction and speed. The transition must feel physically continuous and elegant.

Then, without any cut, the camera passes smoothly through the window glass into the carriage interior in one uninterrupted move. No jump, no teleport, no abrupt zoom, no perspective break. Preserve the same motion vector, same screen direction, and continuous spatial logic.

Inside the carriage, reveal a cozy vintage green train interior with moss-covered seats, warm wooden floor, subtle brass details, soft amber lamps, and a gentle nostalgic atmosphere.

A cute small white blob creature sits quietly by the window. It has a round soft body, tiny rounded ears, simple black dot eyes, and a tiny mouth. It looks calm, gentle, and slightly curious.

The camera continues its smooth lateral movement inside the carriage and settles into a cinematic medium shot of the white creature sitting by the window.

The white creature quietly looks out the window at the forest moving backward outside. The scenery outside the window clearly slides backward with realistic layered parallax. Sunlight flickers softly across the creature, the seat, and the interior walls as the train moves through alternating patches of sun and shade.

The creature stays visually consistent and mostly still, with only very subtle body sway from the train’s motion. The ending shot feels warm, quiet, dreamy, and cinematic.

Style: whimsical animated film aesthetic, hand-painted look, rich green palette, warm golden sunlight, high detail, soft volumetric lighting, realistic parallax, subtle depth of field, gentle motion blur, stable temporal consistency, premium cinematic animation quality.
````
