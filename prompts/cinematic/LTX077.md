## Prompt

````text
Ten seconds, 16:9, one unbroken lateral dolly, ordinary speed with nothing ramped. Late night on a city street in heavy rain, a bar with a large plate glass window across most of its frontage. Starting state: the camera is outside on the sidewalk, level, about two meters back from the glass, looking straight in. Inside, a man in a soaked overcoat sits alone at the far end of the bar with an untouched drink in front of him. Warm tungsten light inside. On the glass there is a blue neon sign from across the street, wet asphalt below it, and headlights passing. 0 to 4 seconds: the camera dollies left to right at a steady walking pace. The interior slides past at one rate and the reflected street slides past at another, both continuously. Rain runs down the outside of the glass throughout. 4 to 7 seconds: a car passes behind the camera. Its headlights travel across the reflection from right to left, crossing over the man's face on the far side of the glass, then leave. He does not react. 7 to 10 seconds: the dolly slows and stops with the man centered. He lifts the drink, does not drink from it, and sets it back down on the same ring of condensation. Camera: one continuous lateral move, left to right, level the whole way, decelerating only in the final block. No pan and no tilt. Glass rule: the window transmits and reflects at the same time for all ten seconds. Interior detail stays visible through it while the neon, the wet street and the passing headlights stay visible on it, each tracking its own source. Hold across the take: the man, the overcoat, the drink and its condensation ring, the bar interior, the neon color and the rain. Sound: rain on pavement and on glass, muffled interior room tone coming through the window, one car passing behind camera with tire noise on wet asphalt, a siren a long way off. Nothing musical. Last frame: dolly stopped, man centered behind the glass, drink back on its ring. Do not: cut, ramp the speed, lose either the reflection or the interior, move the reflection at the same rate as the room, add a second person inside, or put legible lettering on the neon.
````
