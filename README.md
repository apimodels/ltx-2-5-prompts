## How these prompts are written

- **Write one shot as one paragraph, in the order it happens.** The fal.ai examples here each describe a single camera setup from start to finish: shot size and subject, what happens, how the camera moves, the light, and last the lens. "Slow push-in on a boxer … hard overhead spotlights cutting through haze … 50mm at f/2" is the pattern. A pile of keywords gives the model no order to follow.
- **Write the sound, because every clip has it.** LTX-2.5 generates audio together with the picture, and our API has no silent option, so decide what it should sound like. Put spoken lines in quotes and say who speaks, then name the ambience and the one sound that matters. Several Civitai prompts here script a two-person conversation line by line, describing each voice before it speaks.
- **Size the action to a length the API offers.** On Fast, text and image prompts run 6 to 20 seconds in even steps, and only 6, 8 or 10 at 1440p or at 48 / 50 fps; Pro runs 6, 8 or 10. Local workflows often set length as a frame count, so divide it by the frame rate to see what the post asked for and pick the nearest allowed length. The API does not pick a length from your prompt; set duration.
- **With a first frame, describe the motion, not the picture.** In image-to-video the image already fixes the subject, the set and the framing, so spend the prompt on what moves: who turns, what the camera does, what you hear. The clip keeps the image's shape, and sending aspect_ratio together with a first frame returns a 400.
- **For audio-to-video, the track leads and the prompt casts it.** On LTX 2.5 Fast, an audio_url becomes the soundtrack as it is, and the picture is generated to match it. Describe who is on screen, how they move and how the shot is framed, as in our docs example: "A young presenter speaks to the camera in a bright studio, natural hand gestures, medium shot." Length follows the audio unless you set duration. Pro does not take audio.
- **Strip what the API cannot take.** A local LTX-2.5 workflow can carry a negative prompt, LoRA trigger words, extra guide images or a last frame. Our request takes a prompt, one first frame, one audio track and the generation settings, and nothing else: a last frame, reference images or video input return a 400. There is no negative-prompt field either, so anything a post kept there is lost unless you move it into the prompt.
- **Keep on-screen text and chaotic physics off the critical path.** Lightricks' own prompting guide says on-screen text and chaotic physics are still uneven. If a shot depends on a readable sign or a pile of objects colliding, plan for several tries, or add the text in the edit.

Most prompts here were not run on our API: many Civitai entries were made with the open LTX-2.5 weights in local ComfyUI workflows, so the same text can come out differently. On apimodels.app LTX-2.5 runs in two tiers on one endpoint ([API docs](https://apimodels.app/docs/ltx-2-5)):

- [LTX 2.5 Fast](https://apimodels.app/models/ltx-2.5-fast) (`ltx-2.5-fast`) takes a prompt alone, a prompt plus one first frame (`image_url`), or a prompt plus an audio track (`audio_url`), and returns 720p, 1080p or 1440p at 24, 25, 48 or 50 fps. Text and image prompts run 6 to 20 seconds in even steps, and only 6, 8 or 10 seconds at 1440p or at 48 / 50 fps.
- [LTX 2.5 Pro](https://apimodels.app/models/ltx-2.5-pro) (`ltx-2.5-pro`), the tier Lightricks positions for final renders, takes text or a first frame and returns 720p or 1080p at 6, 8 or 10 seconds. It does not take audio.
- A prompt can be up to 5,000 characters. There is no 4K, last frame, reference image or video input, and no negative-prompt field: anything a post kept in a negative prompt is lost unless you move it into the prompt. Sending `aspect_ratio` together with a first frame returns a 400.
- Every clip comes with sound generated together with the picture, and there is no silent option. On Fast, an `audio_url` becomes the soundtrack as it is, and the picture is generated to match it.
- Fast is $0.081 per second at 720p, $0.117 at 1080p and $0.171 at 1440p: 6 seconds at 720p is $0.486, 10 seconds at 1080p $1.17 and 20 seconds at 1080p $2.34. Pro is $0.108 per second at 720p and $0.153 at 1080p, so 10 seconds at 1080p is $1.53. Both are 10% below Lightricks' API list price. Audio-to-video on Fast is billed on the output length to 0.01 s, and failed tasks are not charged.
- A local run may use a dev or distilled checkpoint, its own sampler settings, a negative prompt or extra guide images, and none of that travels with the prompt text. Expect a few tries on Fast or Pro to get close to the clip in the post.
- One prompt here is longer than 5,000 characters and has to be shortened first; the longest is 7,387 characters.
- If you run the open weights yourself, they come under Lightricks' LTX-2.x Community License, which sets its own conditions; read it before any commercial use.

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.apimodels.app/v1/video/generations \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "model": "ltx-2.5-fast",
    "prompt": "Style: high-resolution food photography - close-up - The chocolate is poured over the whipped cream and berries. The chocolate flows slowly, creating rich, glossy streaks across the white cream and the blue blueberry, the deep brown contrasting with the vibrant fruit. A soft, rich sound of chocolate dripping and settling accompanies the pour.",
    "resolution": "1080p",
    "fps": 24,
    "duration": 10,
    "aspect_ratio": "16:9"
  }'
# 10 s at 1080p on Fast: $1.17. The response carries a taskId; poll it until the state is "completed":
curl "https://api.apimodels.app/v1/video/generations?task_id=TASK_ID" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY"
```

## How this collection was made

Prompts come from three kinds of source. Civitai entries are the prompt in the post's public generation data for clips filed under LTX-2.5, kept only when that data lists no LoRA or other add-on model. fal.ai entries are quoted from its LTX-2.5 pages: [the model page](https://fal.ai/ltx-2.5), the Pro text-to-video and image-to-video pages, and two fal.ai guides ([how to use LTX-2.5](https://fal.ai/learn/tools/how-to-use-ltx-2-5) and [Seedance 2.5 vs LTX-2.5 Pro](https://fal.ai/learn/devs/seedance-2-5-vs-ltx-2-5-pro)). Posts on X were found through searches and kept only when the post or its thread names LTX; the prompt is the text in the post itself or in the author's own replies under it.

Left out: posts with no public prompt, posts that are not a usable prompt (ads, tutorials, news, comparisons of several models, chatter, fragments), promotions, prompts mixed with a lot of commentary, sexual content (by keyword, by Civitai's own rating and in review), anything involving minors, real people's likenesses, graphic gore, prompts under 25 characters, and duplicates. On X, a label in front of the prompt such as "Prompt:", trailing hashtags and t.co media links are not part of the stored text; the prompt itself is not edited. Titles and categories are ours, and the IDs follow the order of the library on apimodels.app.
